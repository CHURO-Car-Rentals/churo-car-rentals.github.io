Rent-to-Own

Stop renting.
Start owning.

A low-stakes way into car ownership for anyone tired of paying Avis or Enterprise week after week. No credit check.

  1. 1

    Rent it for a week

    No credit check, so every Rent-to-Own starts with a one-week rental. You get a feel for the car, and anything that needs fixing gets fixed before you sign.

  2. 2

    Sign at MIDFLORIDA

    We meet at MIDFLORIDA to sign and notarize the agreement. You pay the down payment and show proof of insurance: use ours, or your own with CHURO listed as lienholder.

  3. 3

    Pay weekly or bi-weekly

    Fixed payments for up to 12 months. Drive for Uber or DoorDash if you like. You handle routine maintenance, just like an owner.

  4. 4

    It's yours

    Once it's paid off, the car is yours. Drive on with no more rental payments.
